# v2.4.0

## [v2.4.0] - 02-07-2026

Swift v.2.4.0 introduces Customer Center Dashboard, Sign in with Multiple Profiles, redesign of Favorites list and details, Checkout Voucher support, Off-Canvas section types, and a range of bug fixes and accessibility improvements.

> [!NOTE]
> **Requirements**
>
>  **Swift v2.4.0 requires DynamicWeb v10.29 `*` or newer, if Shipmondo is used, it requires Shipmondo.dll version 10.0.5 (installed) or newer**
> **`* Swift v2.x does not run on DynamicWeb 9`**

### Customer Center Roles

The Customer Center serves three distinct B2B roles, each with a different purpose and set of tasks when they log in to the Customer Center. Permissions are set up on the Customer Center pages to show the relevant areas based on which role you are logged in with. 
**On the Demo You can use these roles for login: Salesrep, AccountAdmin, Demouser.**

**B2B Customer**
The everyday buyer. Their focus is on placing orders, tracking orders, managing favorites, and reviewing past purchases — all self-service within their own account.

![alt text](https://doc.dynamicweb.com/Files/Images/Swift/Release-v2.4.0/Demouser.png)

**Sales Rep**
Acts on behalf of customer accounts. They review order history, manage open quotes, and place orders for buyers. Their work spans multiple accounts, so switching context between customers is central to how they use the Customer Center.

![alt text](https://doc.dynamicweb.com/Files/Images/Swift/Release-v2.4.0/Salesrep.png)

**Account Admin**
Manages the company's setup in the Customer Center — users, addresses, and account settings. Their work is administrative rather than transactional.

![alt text](https://doc.dynamicweb.com/Files/Images/Swift/Release-v2.4.0/AccountAdmin.png)


> [!NOTE] Work is ongoing to extend the Customer Center further. Upcoming improvements for later releases include the ability to search and add products directly within Carts and Favorites, as well as support for reordering existing orders

### Customer Center Dashboard
The Customer Center Dashboard has been built to give B2B customers a clearer, more actionable overview of their business the moment they log in.

The dashboard is built around a set of widgets, each focused on a specific area of the B2B workflow:

- **Recent Orders** — a snapshot of the latest completed orders for the account, so buyers can quickly confirm what has shipped and follow up on anything outstanding without leaving the dashboard.
- **Favorites List** — surfaces saved product lists directly on the dashboard, giving buyers one-click access to the products they order most often. Ideal for recurring procurement workflows where speed matters.
- **Monthly Spending Chart** — see how your company's purchasing spend trends over time, making it easier to stay on budget and spot unusual activity early.
- **Pending Quotes** — open quotes are surfaced directly on the dashboard so nothing falls through the cracks. No more navigating into quotes just to check if something needs attention.

![alt text](https://doc.dynamicweb.com/Files/Images/Swift/Release-v2.4.0/B2BCustomerDashboard.png)


> [!IMPORTANT] Pending quotes dashboard widget required a specific quote flow id = QuotePending, in order for it to be shown on the widget
>![alt text](https://doc.dynamicweb.com/Files/Images/Swift/Release-v2.4.0/QuoteFlowState.png)


> [!NOTE] We are focused on the dashboard for B2B customers and will continue to develop the dashboard widgets — for example, a widget showing top purchased products within a given number of days. We also want to create specific dashboard widgets for the Sales Rep and Account Admin.


### Sign in with multiple Profiles
For B2B customers who operate across multiple companies or accounts, logging in is now faster and more intuitive. After selecting a profile, you are taken directly into the right account — no extra redirects or unnecessary steps.

Swift provides two different design templates for the multi profiles.

![alt text](https://doc.dynamicweb.com/Files/Images/Swift/Release-v2.4.0/Multiprofiles1.png)

![alt text](https://doc.dynamicweb.com/Files/Images/Swift/Release-v2.4.0/Multiprofiles2.png)


> [!NOTE] DynamicWeb v10.29 or newer supports multiple profiles sharing the same username (email address). Each profile must have a unique customer number. One profile is designated as the primary login (`isLogin = true`) — this is the account the user lands in by default. From there, they can switch to any of their other profiles. This means a user only needs to remember a single email address and password, regardless of how many accounts they have access to.

### Favorites
Favorites let B2B buyers save the products they come back to regularly, so reordering is fast without having to search or browse each time.

The Favorites list and detail design has been made more consistent: the layout is unified across all views, using the same style as orders and quotes.

Swift provides two different design templates for the favorite list.

![alt text](https://doc.dynamicweb.com/Files/Images/Swift/Release-v2.4.0/FavoriteListList.png)

![alt text](https://doc.dynamicweb.com/Files/Images/Swift/Release-v2.4.0/FavoriteListGrid.png)


> [!NOTE] Planned improvements include saving quantity and units per line, and adding products directly in the Favorites detail.

### Checkout: Voucher
Voucher support has been added to the checkout flow. Customers can apply and remove voucher codes directly in the checkout, with translations and improved error handling.


### GA4: Migrate to dataLayer.push()
All GA4 eCommerce events have been migrated from the deprecated `gtag()` call to `dataLayer.push()`, aligning with the recommended Google Tag Manager integration pattern.

### QueryPublisher: Pagination for PostGrid / PostList
PostGrid and PostList templates rendered via QueryPublisher now support HTMX-based pagination, replacing the previous approach with a simplified page navigation model.
